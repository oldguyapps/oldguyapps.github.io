Apps that keep your information in one place

Written by a retired engineer who wanted apps that just do the job, then share them.

Every Old Guy App makes the same promises:

  • No ads
  • No tracking: nothing is collected or sent to us
  • No in-app purchases: every feature is free
  • Works completely offline; syncs through your own iCloud
  • Your data is yours: back it up any time to a file you keep

The apps

My Weight

A daily weigh-in, a 7-day average that smooths out the day-to-day swings, and a big goal broken into small ones. When you get there, it helps you stay there.

iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch · Coming soon to the App Store

Our Travels

Everything about a trip in one place: flights, things to do before you leave, activities with their reservations and addresses, and a packing list and departure checklist for everyone going. During the trip it shows what's next, on a widget, on your Apple Watch or by asking Siri, and lets you mark a spot so you can find your way back. Share as much or as little as you like with family and friends who use it too.

iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch · Coming soon to the App Store

My Lists

Lists for everything: groceries, hardware, gift ideas. Group items with your own tags, like the aisles of your store, check them off as you go, and search or add from one box at the top. Add a quantity, a note, or a photo of the exact thing you need.

iPhone and iPad · Coming soon to the App Store
