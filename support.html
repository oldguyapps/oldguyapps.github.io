Common questions

My data isn't showing up on my other iPhone or iPad.

The apps sync through your own iCloud account. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, and that iCloud is turned on for the app (Settings › your name › iCloud). Syncing can take a few minutes, especially right after installing.

How do I back up my data?

Each app has Back Up Now in its Settings. The backup is a file you keep, in the Files app: in iCloud Drive in a folder named after the app, or on your device. Settings shows exactly where. Restore from Backup in the same place brings it back.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline. Changes sync to your other devices the next time you're online.

Is there a paid version?

No. Every feature is free for everyone.

My Weight

Why does my goal say "7-day average"?

Your weight swings from day to day with water and meals. Goals are judged on the average of your weigh-ins over the last 7 days (it needs at least 4 of them), so one good or bad morning doesn't decide anything. Settings › How My Weight Works explains the rules.

I missed some days. Is that a problem?

No. Missing days are simply left empty. The app never makes up a number.

Our Travels

Why does the trip screen look different during my trip?

The trip screen follows the trip. While you're planning, tasks, packing and the departure checklist come first. Once your first flight or activity starts, your activities, marked places and photos move to the top.

How do I share a trip with family?

Both of you need Our Travels. In Family & Friends, request a connection using the other person's Apple Account email; they accept it and choose how much you can see. Each trip has its own access settings, and anything marked Private is never shared. Changes reach the other person within a minute or so when you're both online.

How do I mark where I am?

During a trip, tap the pin button on the Home screen and give the spot a name, or say "Mark this location in Our Travels." Your marked places are listed on the trip screen; tap one to get directions back in Apple Maps.

My Lists

I added an item, but it didn't appear twice.

Each list keeps one of each item. If the name is already there, adding it again marks the existing one as needed instead of making a copy.

Where are item photos kept?

A smaller copy of the photo is saved with the item, in the app and your iCloud, and it's included in backups. You can delete the original from your photo library afterward.
