Privacy Policy

The short version: Old Guy Apps don't collect any of your data. We never see it.

Applies to all Old Guy Apps, including My Weight, Our Travels and My Lists. Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. The apps have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and they don't send your information to us or to anyone else.

Where your information is kept

What you enter stays on your device. If iCloud is turned on, it is also stored in your own private iCloud account so it can sync to your other devices. Apple holds that copy under your Apple Account and its own privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Backups

When you choose Back Up Now, the app writes a file in the Files app, in your own iCloud Drive or on your device. That file is yours: it goes only where you put it.

Location, camera, photos and calendar

An app asks for these only when you first use a feature that needs them, and you can say no. Your location is used on your device, for example to mark where you are or to start directions, and is saved only in what you choose to save. Photos you take or pick for an item are stored with that item in the app and your own iCloud. When Our Travels shows the photos you took during a trip, it reads them from your photo library on your device; it doesn't copy or send them anywhere. Calendar events are added to your own calendar. None of this is sent to us.

Siri and Apple Watch

When you use Siri or the Apple Watch app, your request is handled on your devices and in your own iCloud. Nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

Some apps may let you share selected information with family or friends you choose. When they do, it is shared through your iCloud account directly with the people you pick, and only what you choose to share. We never see it.

Children

The apps don't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
